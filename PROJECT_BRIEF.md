## Project Goal

Build a modern, professional, mobile-friendly website that helps Theo’s Mobile Auto Detailing attract customers, present services clearly, and receive quote requests.

The website should not look like a basic flyer. It should feel premium, trustworthy, clean, and easy to use.

## Target Customers

- Car owners
- SUV and truck owners
- Families
- Pet owners
- RV owners
- Boat owners
- Commercial vehicle owners
- Fleet customers

## Main Website Goals

1. Present the business professionally.
2. Show the three main detailing packages.
3. Display all additional services by category.
4. Make it easy for customers to call or request a quote.
5. Build trust through photos, reviews, and clear pricing notes.
6. Work perfectly on mobile devices.

## Main Packages

### Basic Detail

Starting prices:

- Sedans: starting at $180
- Larger vehicles: starting at $195

Estimated completion time:

- 2–3 hours

Includes:

- Dash & console cleaned
- Wipe down all interior surfaces
- Vacuum carpets, mats & trunk/cargo area
- Windows cleaned inside and outside
- Door jambs cleaned
- Hand wash
- Wheel wells & rims cleaned
- Tail pipes cleaned
- Gas cap area cleaned
- Tires dressed

### Deluxe Detail

Starting prices:

- Sedans: starting at $280
- Larger vehicles: starting at $320

Estimated completion time:

- 3–4 hours

Includes everything in Basic Detail, plus:

- Clay bar treatment
- Wax

### Platinum Detail

Starting prices:

- Sedans: starting at $450
- Larger vehicles: starting at $500

Estimated completion time:

- 4–8 hours

Includes everything in Deluxe Detail, plus:

- UV protectant on dash
- Shampoo carpets & mats
- Shampoo seats or recondition leather seats
- Spot clean headliner

## Pricing Notes

Prices depend on vehicle size and condition.

Extra charges apply for:

- Extra dirty vehicles
- Pet hair
- Special conditions requiring more time or labor
